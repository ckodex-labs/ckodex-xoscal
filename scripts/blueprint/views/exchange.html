Exchange doctrine

XOSCAL Exchange is a verification substrate, not a share button.

  • Publishing does not imply trusted.
  • Receiving does not imply verified.
  • Verification is the receiver's, re-run locally.
  • Reconciliation is by hash, not custody transfer.
  • Disclosure requires explicit class.
  • Inbound claims enter as imported-unverified until VerifyClaim passes locally.
