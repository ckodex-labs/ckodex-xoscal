Evidence Cartography — the card system

Every external artifact becomes a card only when it can trace to an OSCAL object, claim, assessment result, back-matter resource, mapping edge, or declared extension manifest. No card may be decorative.

Canonical card families

Control — Claim — Evidence — Finding — Gap — Conflict — POA&M — Mapping Edge — Source — Export Bundle. Each card answers: what is this, what does it claim, what does it support, what is its proof state, what can safely happen next.
